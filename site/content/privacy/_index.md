**Effective date: September 7, 2026**

Wellness After 40 ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit wellness-after-40.netlify.app (the "Site"), how we use it, and what choices you have.

### Information we collect

**Newsletter signups.** If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address (and, if you provide it, your name) through our signup form. This information is stored and processed by our email service provider, GetResponse, on our behalf.

**Contact form.** If you use the Contact page to send us a message, we collect your name, email address, and whatever message you write. These submissions are handled through Netlify Forms.

**Automatically collected information.** Like most websites, our hosting provider (Netlify) may automatically log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

We do not currently run analytics tracking (such as Google Analytics) on the Site. If that changes, we'll update this policy to describe what's collected and how to opt out.

### How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

- Send you the newsletter content, tips, and occasional offers you signed up for
- Respond to messages you send through the Contact form
- Maintain and improve the Site
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

### Third parties we work with

- **GetResponse** (email marketing platform) — stores your email address and sends the newsletter on our behalf. See [GetResponse's Privacy Policy](https://www.getresponse.com/legal/privacy) for details on how they handle data.
- **Netlify** (website hosting and form handling) — hosts the Site and processes Contact form submissions. See [Netlify's Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) for details.
### Affiliate links

Some links on this Site are affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you click through and make a purchase, at no extra cost to you. See our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for details. Clicking an affiliate link may result in the destination site setting its own cookies or collecting information under its own privacy policy — we don't control that.

### Your choices

- **Unsubscribe** — every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time.
- **Access or deletion** — to ask what information we hold about you, or to have it deleted, contact us via the [Contact page](/contact/).

### Children's privacy

This Site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above will reflect the most recent change.
